# Vertex Lesson Template

## 1. Lesson Goal

Defines what the learner should understand or be able to do by the end of the lesson. This section keeps the lesson focused on one clear learning outcome.

## 2. Why It Matters

Explains why the topic is useful in real software engineering work. This should connect the lesson to practical decisions, workflows, or problems developers encounter.

## 3. Core Concept

Introduces the main idea of the lesson in a concise and structured way. This section should explain the concept enough for the learner to apply it, without turning the lesson into theory-heavy reading.

## 4. Mental Model

Gives the learner a simple way to reason about the concept. The goal is to help them predict behavior, debug mistakes, and explain the idea in their own words.

## 5. Guided Walkthrough

Shows the learner how to apply the concept step by step. This section should reduce ambiguity before asking the learner to work independently.

## 6. Common Mistakes

Highlights frequent beginner errors, misconceptions, or poor habits related to the topic. This helps learners recognize problems before they become repeated patterns.

## 7. Practice Task

Defines a focused task that requires the learner to apply the lesson. The task should be small enough to complete in one sitting and concrete enough to verify.

## 8. Self-Check

Lists the criteria the learner can use to evaluate their own work. This section should make completion depend on understanding and correctness, not just finishing.

## 9. Reflection

Prompts the learner to explain what they did, what changed, and why it worked. Reflection supports durable understanding and prepares learners for technical communication.

## 10. Next Step

Connects the lesson to the next topic, task, or project milestone. This keeps the learning path coherent and reinforces forward progress.
